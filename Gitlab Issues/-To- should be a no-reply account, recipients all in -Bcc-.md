### "To" should be a no-reply account, recipients all in "Bcc"
##### Due on 

Some Alerts are sent to a large collection of people. Those should set the Recipients in the `BCC` field with a no-reply address as the `TO`.

Other Alerts get sent to specific Recipients per the data content of the Alert. When there's only 1 Recipient, use the `TO` field.

In a later revision, we may want to divide the fields by Manager/Report hierarchy. Just a thought.
