### Preset criteria for BCP that need SFA links
##### Due on 

BC Projects are initially grouped into two sets for SF Account pairing:

### Set 1:

* Value: **$25 - 100M**
* Facility Type: **Acute Care Hospital | Ambulatory Surgery Center | Cancer Center | Medical Office | Rehab Facility | Specialty Clinic | Urgent Care/CommunityClinic**
* Stage: **Planning | Starts in \[1-3, 4-12, 12+\] Months | Groundbreaking | Early Construction | Construction | Finalizing Construction | On Hold/Postponed**
* Date: Currently unclear how this functionally affects workflow, but documentation states

  > Date should be based on Construction End Date of date that upload is taking place and two years from that date.

  Example shows `Construction End Date: 03/02/2023` and end of range `03/02/2025`

### Set 2:

* Value: \>**$100M**
* Facility Type: Same criteria
* Stage: Same criteria
* Date: Currently unclear. Criteria is the same as above, only extending end-of-range to 4 years.
